[Day 37, 12 in the afternoon]
After entering the Soul Cairn, Serana and Soul quipped about the realm's foul atmosphere and the bonemen's lack of loot, then spotted four more bonemen with Lydia already engaging them. Upon finding Serana's mother Valerica, she accused Soul of being a vampire hunter, but Serana defended him, explaining he had earned her trust by helping her through crypts and even following her into the Soul Cairn. Valerica remained wary but eventually revealed they need to deal with a dragon named Durnehviir to lower the barrier—Serana joked about the absurdity of a dragon in the soul-wasting land and suggested violence over negotiation.
