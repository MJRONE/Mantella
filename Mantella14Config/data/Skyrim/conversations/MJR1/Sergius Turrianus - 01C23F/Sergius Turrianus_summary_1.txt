[Day 34, 10 at night]
Ancano remarked that the storm outside was a fitting reflection of the College's pathetic grasp on the arcane, while Sergius Turrianus and the player remained silent in the Hall of the Elements amidst the blizzard.

[Day 38, 1 in the afternoon]
In the Arcanaeum, Urag gro-Shub explained to MJR that the College doesn't care enough to pursue Orthorn, an unskilled apprentice who stole supplies and books to ingratiate himself with a group of renegade mages who were "persuaded to leave" due to their forbidden research at Fellglow Keep. When MJR inquired about an Elder Scroll, Urag scoffed at the notion, claiming even the greatest thief couldn't touch one under his security. Daegon sarcastically remarked that Orthorn sounded like the type of fool who'd steal from a library and end up dead by his own experiments, while Sergius Turrianus dismissively noted the College's laughable security. Urag then directed MJR to Septimus Signus, a master of Elder Scroll lore who disappeared years ago in the northern ice fields near a Dwemer artifact, and handed over limited, unreliable information on the matter.
