[Day 58, 8 in the morning]
Mallus Maccius outlined his plan to MJR at Honningbrew Meadery: poison the mead for Sabjorn's tasting of Whiterun's guard captain, using pest poison Sabjorn would unknowingly provide. Mallus revealed he had unboarded a tunnel entrance in the warehouse basement to infest the meadery, then directed MJR to clear the pests while dumping the poison into the brewing vat. He admitted his debt to Sabjorn drove him, but the real payoff was Maven Black-Briar making him manager of the meadery once Sabjorn ended up in jail, with Lydia and Daegon listening nearby as the scheme unfolded.
