[Day 43, 10 at night]
Blaise and the Haafingar Guard chat in Solitude, with the stable boy curious about the guard's life—asking about boredom on the wall, torches going out in rain, wolves threatening the city, and whether he ever leaves the gates. The guard explains his duties, the constant vigilance against dangers like wolves and bandits, and remarks that his life is better than sleeping in a stable.

[Day 44, 0 at night]
At Katla’s Farm under a clear night sky, the Carriage Driver remarked on the fine weather, the traveler noted the dark’s appeal, and Blaise admitted he rarely sleeps due to restless thoughts and horses. When the guard’s comment about preferring the wall to a stable came up, the driver noted the warmth of animals, while the traveler dismissed warmth as overrated and asked if Blaise ever thought of leaving—Blaise said he did, but had nowhere to go.
