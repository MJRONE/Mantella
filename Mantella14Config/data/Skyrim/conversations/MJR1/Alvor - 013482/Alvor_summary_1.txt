[Day 18, 11 at night]
At the Sleeping Giant Inn in Riverwood's cozy glow, Lydia loyally guarded her Thane MJR during trades with Orgnar, praising Dibella's favor and Nord purity while distrusting Delphine's probing questions about the retrieved Horn of Jurgen Windcaller; Alvor, the local blacksmith, offered his forge for gear repairs amid tales of dragons stirring in Kynesgrove, affirming Skyrim's defenses against Thalmor threats and foreign taint as the group planned the perilous trek under the night's chill.
