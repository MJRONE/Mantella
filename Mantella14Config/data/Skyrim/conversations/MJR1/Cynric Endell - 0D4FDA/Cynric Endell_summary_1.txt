Cynric Endell, Brynjolf, Daegon, and the group arrived at the Thieves Guild Headquarters, where Vipir the Fleet and Thrynn were nearby. Serana and Lydia stood close to Mercer Frey, and everyone prepared to discuss their next move or debrief on recent events.
