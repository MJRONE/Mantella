[Day 52, 5 in the early morning]
The Solitude Guard and Jaree-Ra conclude their tense nighttime exchange in a cobblestone courtyard near Solitude's docks. The guard presses Jaree-Ra about a missing Argonian cargo ship, but Jaree-Ra deflects, hinting the vessel had heavy debts to dangerous parties and offering to sell the name of those responsible. The guard grows suspicious of Jaree-Ra's knowledge. Jaree-Ra reflects on the stars, noting the difference between Solitude's cold, distant sky and the humid canopies of Black Marsh. The atmosphere remains somber and shadowed, with ancient stone walls, a dark red banner, and dim lantern light framing their veiled confrontation.
