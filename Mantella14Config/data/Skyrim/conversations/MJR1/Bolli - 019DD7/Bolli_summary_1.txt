[Day 45, 0 at night]
Vulwulf Snow-Shod and Bolli commiserated over their unsatisfactory marriages, with Bolli complaining about Nivenor's spending and lack of affection while Vulwulf noted his wife's frequent visits to the Temple of Dibella. They discussed Asgeir's wasteful habits, Sapphire's sharp tongue toward Marcurio, and the moral decline of Riften, with Bolli hinting at Haelga's influence and Vulwulf speculating about gambling in the Bee and Barb before Bolli excused himself.

[Day 57, 1 at night]
In the dimly lit Bee and Barb, Vulwulf Snow-Shod commented to Bolli that the fire was dying and the hour late, with few patrons left besides the usual regulars, to which Bolli nodded, noting Asgeir still nursing his tankard nearby.

[Day 58, 10 in the morning]
In Riften's morning light, Serana quipped about the charming blend of fish, corruption, and regret in the air, while Bolli noted Maven Black-Briar's dangerous expression meant someone was about to lose more than their business. Shadr commented that Sabjorn's mead had been going sour for months, and Bolli agreed that sour deals and sour mead went hand in hand in Riften, preferring fish that only smell bad fresh out of the water. Serana finished by suggesting the symbol on the promissory note looked like it was drawn by someone with trembling hands and a guilty conscience—or perhaps just the standard handwriting of people who owe Maven money.
