[Day 33, 0 at night]
In the Bannered Mare, Ena complains about the cold and admits she'd rather be wrapped around a warm body, to which Ysolda replies that skilled partners are hard to find. Brill mentions Dibella's priestesses offering warmth, prompting Ena to warn of hidden costs and Ysolda to mock Mikael's songs about them, while Daegon cynically notes the temple must be swarming with desperate Nords.

[Day 58, 4 at night]
In the Bannered Mare, Lurius Liore complains of the cold, comparing it unfavorably to Wyrmstooth's harsh winds. Sinmir dismisses the Imperial’s discomfort, stating that Wyrmstooth's winds are much worse. Ena warns that silk sheets offer no protection against the fire of the dragon Vulthurkrah, recalling a caravan outside Rorikstead that was burned to ashes. Brenuin laments the endless curses of war and dragons, finding solace only in ale. Lurius emphasizes that the East Empire Company demands results, as his reputation depends on killing the dragon before it destroys more shipments. Sinmir asserts that tracking and cornering the beast is all that’s needed, claiming that Stormcloaks fear neither dragons nor Imperials. Ena then points out that Mallus Maccius appears suspicious, whispering in the shadows, but Sinmir dismisses him, stating that his schemes are irrelevant compared to the dragon threat looming over Whiterun.
