[Day 43, 10 at night]
Blaise and the Haafingar Guard chat in Solitude, with the stable boy curious about the guard's life—asking about boredom on the wall, torches going out in rain, wolves threatening the city, and whether he ever leaves the gates. The guard explains his duties, the constant vigilance against dangers like wolves and bandits, and remarks that his life is better than sleeping in a stable.

[Day 59, 3 at night]
After MJR pressed Gulum-Ei for more details about the anonymous buyer of the Firebrand Wine, the Argonian merchant insisted he had no further information and left the Winking Skeever. Minutes later, as the night grew still, the Solitude and Haafingar Guards remarked on the unusual late-night business, and Gulum-Ei—now walking under the stars—replied that a merchant's work never truly ends, even under Dibella's moon.
