[Day 52, 2 at night]
In the warm interior of Candlehearth Hall, Lydia expressed her relief at escaping the cold, while Luaffyn offered to play a song to thaw her Nord heart. The conversation turned to Captain Lonely-Gale, a brooding patron rumored to be haunted by a failed Solstheim expedition, and then to a killer on the loose in Windhelm. Lydia blamed Dunmer squatters in the Gray Quarter, but Luaffyn noted the victims were Nords, suggesting a vampire or the busybody Viola as culprits. Lydia dismissed the bard's teasing about her overprotectiveness and set off to find MJR, worried about him walking alone with a murderer about.
