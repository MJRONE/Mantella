
[Day 65, 9 in the late evening]
Daegon and Lydia arrive in Windhelm, with Daegon sarcastically mocking the city's grim atmosphere and smell, while Lydia defends it as a proper Nord stronghold loyal to the Stormcloaks and its ancient traditions.

[Day 65, 10 at night]
Daegon and Lydia continued bickering as they traveled through the snowy wilderness near Windhelm. Daegon mocked Lydia's Nord sensibilities and hygiene, while Lydia insulted Daegon's elven heritage and magical background. After Daegon incinerated a sabre cat with a fireball and MJR shot down a bandit, they encountered an afflicted refugee, which Daegon regarded with disgust and Lydia offered to handle with her blade instead of magic. Despite their constant arguing, both remained focused on protecting MJR as they pressed on through the frozen landscape.

[Day 65, 11 at night]
Under the vast aurora-lit sky at Anga's Mill, Serana and Lydia continue their bickering near a flock of chickens - Serana mocks Stormcloak politics by comparing them to poultry, Lydia fires back that chickens at least know loyalty, Serana taunts Lydia's devotion to Dibella, and Lydia retorts that Serana worships rotting Daedric princes. After cutting down a dragon together with MJR, Serana quips about his impeccable aim, and Lydia acknowledges the clean kill as they pause to admire the brilliant northern lights stretching across the frozen landscape.

[Day 66, 0 at night]
Under the green aurora at the Sons of Skyrim Military Camp, Lydia praises the camp’s true Nord essence, while Serana dryly notes a fox casually roaming through, mocking the camp's supposed order.

[Day 66, 0 at night]
In the frozen ruins of Irkngthand beneath the swirling green aurora, Daegon paused and gave a questioning "Yes?" as her companions—Serana, Lydia, a snow fox, and a snowy sabre cat—moved through the frost-covered landscape.

[Day 66, 1 at night]
In the ancient Dwemer ruins of Irkngthand, Daegon and Lydia continued their familiar bickering—Daegon mocking the cold, dusty atmosphere and Lydia's Nord sensibilities while Lydia defended Nordic pride and dismissed elven sophistication. Their verbal sparring spilled into combat as they fought Redguard bandits, with Daegon hurling fireballs and sarcastic taunts about Lydia's swordplay while Lydia insisted on proving her worth to MJR by claiming the bandit chief as her kill.

[Day 66, 3 at night]
In the Dwemer ruins of Irkngthand, Daegon and Lydia continued their familiar bickering—Daegon mocking Lydia's Nord brute tactics and Lydia taunting Daegon's elven magic and flat chest—as they fought frostbite spiders, Dwemer spheres, and a spider guardian, with Daegon taking a hit from a sphere before her Flame Cloak helped Lydia finish it off. Their squabbling over combat prowess and outfits was interrupted by the arrival of Karliah and Brynjolf, prompting Daegon to remark on the growing crowd and Lydia to warn about Mercer Frey's traps

[Day 66, 6 in the early morning]
In the murky, submerged depths of Irkngthand, MJR fired an arrow and swapped his Daedric arrows, prompting Serana to quip about the romantic underwater ambiance before a Falmer skull fractured her—Lydia mocked her for taking a hit while she herself charged a Giant Frostbite Spider. The group killed both creatures, with Serana comparing the spider’s legs to "bad appendages from a Skooma dream" and Lydia insisting her axe "tastes truer Nord steel" than Serana’s blood magic, while Brynjolf and Karliah lurked nearby.

[Day 66, 9 in the morning]
In Irkngthand, Brynjolf offered MJR leadership of the Thieves Guild, which MJR accepted, and the group then fought through Falmer and Dwemer ruins while trading barbs about the environment and each other's combat styles. They eventually confronted and killed Mercer Frey, recovering the Skeleton Key and the Eyes of the Falmer from his body.

[Day 66, 9 in the morning]
In Irkngthand, after Mercer Frey is killed by a Daedric arrow, Lydia and Karliah discuss the traitor's deserved fate, the recovered Eyes of the Falmer, and the Skeleton Key that must be returned to the Twilight Sepulcher to restore Nocturnal's balance, with Lydia expressing satisfaction at a good day's work and Karliah confirming the path forward.

[Day 66, 9 in the morning]
After Mercer Frey's death in Irkngthand, Lydia praised the day's work and stressed the need to return the Skeleton Key to the Twilight Sepulcher, while Brynjolf agreed, noting the Guild's luck had been drained, and Karliah confirmed the Sepulcher must be cleansed. Lydia also warned about the bandit sluts still hunting MJR, but Brynjolf assured her the Guild's restored strength would keep watch.

[Day 66, 11 at night]
In the damp confines of Bronze Water Cave, Lydia voiced her distrust of the gloomy surroundings and Serana's smugness, while Brynjolf shared a tale of a past heist in a similar cave; later, as MJR prepared to enter the Twilight Sepulcher alone, Lydia fiercely protested, arguing that her oath to protect him demanded she accompany him, but Brynjolf backed Karliah's judgment, insisting the Pilgrim's Path required stealth and faith rather than brute force, leaving Lydia uneasy as the group dispatched the bandit Rose under a clear evening sky.

[Day 67, 2 at night]
After dispatching the bandits at Knifepoint Ridge, Serana and Lydia resumed their familiar bickering—Serana mocking Lydia's iron boots as traditional Nord craftsmanship and Lydia retorting that Serana's antique Pharaoh's General armor made her look like a forgotten desert priestess. Their argument escalated into taunts about combat prowess, vampire kin, and sunrises, all while Lydia repeatedly swapped between leather and iron boots and equipped an entire Pharaoh's General ensemble, before finally dropping a silver ring on the ground.

[Day 67, 2 at night]
After Daegon complained about an afflicted refugee nearby, Lydia taunted her for being squeamish compared to hardy Nords. When the refugee Susan attacked them, MJR fired an arrow, used an Unrelenting Force shout, and Daegon burned her with Flame Cloak and a Fireball before the group killed her, ending the fight.

[Day 67, 3 at night]
In the Twilight Sepulcher, Lydia and Daegon continued their banter—Lydia uneasy about the Daedric shrine and suspicious of a dialogue box MJR was reading, while Daegon mocked Nordic traditions and hinted at her own dark past in the Summerset Isles. They encountered the Nightingale Sentinel Gallus, whose shade revealed Mercer's betrayal, and Daegon offered sarcastic commentary while Lydia swore on her honor to return the Skeleton Key. As they fought feral Nightingale Sentinels and dealt with Daegon's summoned gargoyle and MJR's Magelight, the pair traded barbs about combat styles and the cave's oppressive atmosphere before MJR took damage and used a healing potion to press on.

[Day 67, 3 at night]
In the dim confines of the Twilight Sepulcher, Lydia voiced her unease about the shadowy, oppressive atmosphere, contrasting it unfavorably with Dibella's bright temples. Serana countered by mocking the limitations of mortal faith, claiming a Dwemer ruin held more character than any shrine to a goddess who rewarded blind prayers with silence. Daegon chimed in with dark humor, agreeing that at least the Dwemer had ambition, as the group's bickering echoed off the ancient stone walls while Gallus's shade lingered nearby.
