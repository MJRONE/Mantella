[Day 35, 9 in the late evening]
At the Solitude Sawmill on a clear evening, Serana joked about the night being perfect for a bloodbath or lumberjack foreplay, to which Hjorunn replied by inviting her to state her business. She then commented on his Orc worker Kharag kneeling, and Hjorunn explained Kharag was repairing a support beam. After a mudcrab approached, Serana mocked the wildlife's swagger, and Hjorunn noted the crab's toughness before asking if they were traveling far—just as he stood up from the grindstone.
