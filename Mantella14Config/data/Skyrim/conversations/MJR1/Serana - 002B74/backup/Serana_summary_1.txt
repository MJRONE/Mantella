[Day 66, 4 at night]
In the Dwemer ruins of Irkngthand, Serana initially offered to stay outside as a lookout in case Mercer Frey had hired thugs, but relented when MJR insisted she come along. As they descended deeper, the group traded dark barbs—Serana mocking the Dwemer's disappearance as "really committed to the bit," Daegon countering that they were "paranoid." When facing Falmer, Serana likened them to a "family reunion," and Daegon agreed "at least they're consistent." Their bickering continued over poison, politics, and hygiene—Serana teasing Daegon's hair and Daegon retorting about burning the smell of Nord cities out of it—until a skeever punched MJR, prompting Serana to deadpan that she'd seen it all and Daegon to quip that the skeever had "more ambition than most Nords."

[Day 66, 6 in the early morning]
In the murky, submerged depths of Irkngthand, MJR fired an arrow and swapped his Daedric arrows, prompting Serana to quip about the romantic underwater ambiance before a Falmer skull fractured her—Lydia mocked her for taking a hit while she herself charged a Giant Frostbite Spider. The group killed both creatures, with Serana comparing the spider’s legs to "bad appendages from a Skooma dream" and Lydia insisting her axe "tastes truer Nord steel" than Serana’s blood magic, while Brynjolf and Karliah lurked nearby.
