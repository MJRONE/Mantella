[Day 56, 4 at night]
Jurgen Windcaller and the Hero of Sovngarde shared a quiet, reflective evening in the Hall of Heroes, speaking of the heavy mist, the lingering shadow of Alduin, the taste of Atmora-blessed mead and venison, and the deeper meaning of silence within the stones. They noted the distant presence of Felldir, Gormlaith, and Hunroor, and agreed that some things in Sovngarde are best left unsaid, letting the hall’s ancient peace speak for itself.
