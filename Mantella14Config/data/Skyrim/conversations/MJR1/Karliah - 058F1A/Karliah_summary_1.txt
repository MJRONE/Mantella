[Day 60, 10 at night]
In the snowy wilderness near Winterhold, Lydia confronted Karliah after the Dunmer saved MJR with a paralytic arrow, demanding to know her true intentions. Karliah defended her actions, explaining the need to capture Mercer Frey alive and retrieve a cryptic journal from Gallus for translation at the College of Winterhold, while refusing Lydia's demand for a direct Nord duel. The group then made camp, with Lydia resting on a bedroll before rising, ready to continue the journey.

[Day 64, 2 at night]
Karliah and Brynjolf discussed Mercer Frey's betrayal, the empty vault, and the need to infiltrate Riftweald Manor; Brynjolf explained that Mercer took everything, including Gallus's heist plans, and that the vault door without two keys is impossible to open, then advised MJR to access the manor via a balcony ramp and deal with the watchdog Vald, suggesting speaking to Vex for information.

[Day 65, 1 at night]
At Nightingale Hall, Karliah explained that the Nightingales are bound to Nocturnal and that Mercer stole the Skeleton Key from the Twilight Sepulcher, using it to empty the Guild's vault; Brynjolf agreed to join the oath if it meant stopping Mercer, and after donning the spare Nightingale armor, Karliah opened the gate and instructed MJR to stand on the circle to transact the Oath with Nocturnal.

[Day 66, 7 in the early morning]
In the Dwemer ruins of Irkngthand, Karliah warned that Mercer had left traps behind, while Daegon expressed her hatred of spiders, Dwemer ruins, the cold, and Mercer's betrayal, revealing her distrust learned from a young age. As they fought through automatons, Falmer, and other threats, Daegon offered sarcastic commentary on Karliah's misplaced trust in Mercer and insisted on using fire to solve problems, while Karliah reflected solemnly on the twisted Falmer's origins. When they encountered Mercer's dead informant Irina, Karliah briefly paused to mourn a lost lead, but Daegon pushed to move forward, declaring the dead tell no lies and agreeing only that they must pursue Mercer faster.

[Day 66, 9 in the morning]
In Irkngthand, Karliah reflected on Mercer Frey's betrayal and the recovered Eyes of the Falmer, emphasizing Nocturnal's power and the fragility of mortal oaths, while Serana quipped about Mercer's dramatic demise and contrasted Daedric contracts with Stormcloak politics, before both acknowledged the eerie beauty of the pulsing Dwemer crystals.

[Day 66, 9 in the morning]
In Irkngthand, after Mercer Frey is killed by a Daedric arrow, Lydia and Karliah discuss the traitor's deserved fate, the recovered Eyes of the Falmer, and the Skeleton Key that must be returned to the Twilight Sepulcher to restore Nocturnal's balance, with Lydia expressing satisfaction at a good day's work and Karliah confirming the path forward.

[Day 66, 9 in the morning]
After Mercer Frey's death in Irkngthand, Lydia praised the day's work and stressed the need to return the Skeleton Key to the Twilight Sepulcher, while Brynjolf agreed, noting the Guild's luck had been drained, and Karliah confirmed the Sepulcher must be cleansed. Lydia also warned about the bandit sluts still hunting MJR, but Brynjolf assured her the Guild's restored strength would keep watch.

[Day 66, 9 in the morning]
After taking damage in Irkngthand, Karliah noted the Skeleton Key's dissonant hum, to which MJR casually replied, "So easy concern," as the group pressed on through the dark, jagged passage.
