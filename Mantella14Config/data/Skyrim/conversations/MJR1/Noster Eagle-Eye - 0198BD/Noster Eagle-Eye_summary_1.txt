[Day 52, 4 at night]
In Solitude, Lydia expressed her distrust of the city's fancy folk and guards, while Noster Eagle-Eye shared his experiences sleeping in back alleys and warned her about Jaree-Ra skimming coin from the docks. After discussing the beauty of the aurora and the potential usefulness of Sorex's rum errand to the Blue Palace, Lydia noticed MJR shedding his armor and approaching two painted women under the aurora. Alarmed, she declared she would intervene with her greatsword, dismissing Noster's flattery about her looks and his warning to watch her back, before marching off to confront the situation.

[Day 59, 0 at night]
At the Winking Skeever, Sorex Vinius gave MJR a bottle of Stros M'Kai Rum to deliver to Falk Firebeard at the Blue Palace, saving him the trouble. When MJR asked if he worked there, Sorex initially claimed ownership but then admitted his father Corpulus actually owns the inn, though it will be his someday.

[Day 59, 3 at night]
In the streets of Solitude late at night, Solitude Guard questioned two travelers about their business, while Noster Eagle-Eye remarked that trouble follows some folk closer than their own shadow and that he merely watches from the shadows as a broken scout with one eye and empty pockets. Daegon sarcastically noted that broken scouts and bored guards make for delightful company, then pointed to the Blue Palace, describing it as full of Jarls and sycophants polishing Dibella's statues while pretending not to stare at guards in their Imperial Light Bikini armor. The Guard retorted that the armor was regulation for the night watch, keeping them nimble and intimidating, and claimed the goddess appreciated the devotion. Noster recalled Jaree-Ra skimming coin from the docks, but the Guard insisted the Argonian had been clean for months. After Noster noticed a rabbit wandering near the walls and Daegon joked it could be a werewolf or Thalmor agent, Noster pointed out the warm light of the Winking Skeever and lamented his fall from Legion scout to counting coins thrown by drunks. Daegon then spotted a loose horse and suggested it was either a careless stable hand or a planned quick exit
