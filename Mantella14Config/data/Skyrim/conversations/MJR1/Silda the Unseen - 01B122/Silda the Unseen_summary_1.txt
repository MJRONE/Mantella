[Day 52, 2 at night]
In the frozen streets of Windhelm under a vivid aurora, Daegon remarked that the city felt like a tomb, to which Silda the Unseen replied that one grows accustomed to the cold and silence, valuing a warm body when found; Daegon insisted she prefers fire she controls over fleeting charity, and Silda warned that nothing in Windhelm is free—everything has a price.
