[Day 44, 2 at night]
Lydia and Tsavani talk amidst the chaos: Lydia boasts of slaying Thalmor soldiers and condemns their presence in Skyrim, while Tsavani pleads for her life and offers stew. They spot Elenwen, and Lydia thirsts for her blood, but Tsavani warns of torture. After defeating a frost atronach, Lydia admires the sky painted by Dibella, and Tsavani thanks her for mercy, suggesting a warm meal.
